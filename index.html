Why this exists

Camp lessons were built for one kind of kid.

  1. 01

    Too young for the teens

    Most lessons were written for little kids. A 16-year-old building the same straw bridge as a 7-year-old checks out fast.

  2. 02

    Same lesson, every summer

    Returning campers have seen it before. Without a way to mix things up, the surprise is gone by week one.

  3. 03

    It feels like school

    Worksheets and lectures belong in September. Summer should be loud, visual and a little bit competitive.

  4. 04

    One story for every group

    Campers bring different interests. A lesson framed around their world lands harder than one framed around nobody’s.

For counselors

From zero to running a lesson in a minute.

  1. Tell it about your group

    Age group, topic, what they’re into, how long you have and how many campers.

  2. Get a fresh lesson

    The generator mixes story, mission and showdown pieces, and skips ones your browser has already shown.

  3. Present it full-screen

    Hit play. Arrow keys or clicks move through the slides. No PowerPoint needed.

What funding unlocks

This demo runs for free today. With support, it grows up.

  • Counselor accounts so each camp keeps its own lesson history.
  • AI-written lessons on demand for any topic and theme.
  • Easier / harder buttons to adjust a lesson on the spot.
  • Camper ratings so the best lessons rise to the top.
  • New topics starting with coding and tech.
